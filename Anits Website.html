Welcome to ANITS

Anil Neerukonda Institute of Technology & Sciences (ANITS), Visakhapatnam is owned by Megha Engineering and Infrastructure Limited (MEIL), one of India's leading infrastructure and manufacturing companies.

The institute was established during the academic year 2001-02 by Anil Neerukonda Educational Society (ANES) founded by Dr. N.B.R. Prasad. The college provides quality engineering education with modern laboratories, experienced faculty, excellent placement opportunities, and state-of-the-art infrastructure to prepare students for successful careers.
